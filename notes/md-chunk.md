# Claude MD Chunk

**Date:** 06/04/2026

A CLI tool that prunes bloated `CLAUDE.md` files down to essentials and offloads supplementary detail into a dedicated `agent-context/` folder. The goal is the same as the split-context pattern: keep the always-loaded context lean and load topic-specific material on demand.

This is the automated counterpart to manually splitting CLAUDE.md.

**Repo:** https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-MD-Chunk
